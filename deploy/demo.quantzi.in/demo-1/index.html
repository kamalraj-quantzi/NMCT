Who We Are

Born in Coimbatore. Built for the underserved.

Registered on 13 September 1988 under the Indian Trust Act of 1882, NMCT grew from a research-driven idea into a development force — aligning every intervention with the Sustainable Development Goals, and standing with underprivileged children, women, migrant workers, tribal communities and people living with HIV/AIDS.

Vision

Sustainable holistic development among the underserved.

Mission

Capacity building, promotion and support services towards socio-economic empowerment.
